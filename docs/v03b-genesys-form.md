# Customer Service - AI Scoring form recreation

The AQM form `genesys_customer_service_ai_scoring` recreates the real Genesys quality form shown in Simon's 29 September 2026 TextEdit photo and approximate OCR. It is an **AQM adaptation**, not a round-trip import or an assertion of exact Genesys scoring parity. The source form identifier visible in the OCR is retained as provenance; personal user and dispute-assignee details are omitted.

The form has 17 visible prompts across **Opening** (6), **Assisting The Customer** (3), **Call Handling Skills** (3), and **Compliance & Closure** (5). It retains the observed section labels, the source's 100/100/100/1000 group-weight values as reference metadata, Yes/No values of 5/0 or 1/0 where visible, three 1–5 scales, and the source's Not Applicable eligibility on relevant questions. DPA validation and appropriate solution are marked as critical; the source's DPA `isKill` flag is represented by AQM's critical-question behavior because AQM has no distinct kill rule.

The approximate OCR and the photo do not fully establish all original context IDs, visibility predicates, group-weight calculations, and scoring settings. AQM stores its own stable question IDs and normalizes answer credit to 0–1. It currently scores enabled questions with equal AQM weight rather than recreating Genesys' `SCALED` group-weight algorithm. The call-reason prompt was a multi-select classification in Genesys; AQM keeps it visible but disabled because its existing choice type is single-select. The products/services question has an unresolved source visibility condition and is also disabled. Several other questions remain visible but disabled because the screenshot's `aiScoring.questionSettings` did not clearly enable them.

**The form itself is disabled by default.** Review its wording, answer options, conditions, and scoring against the original Genesys JSON before enabling it for a paid evaluation. Existing browser-local form libraries receive this form once via a migration marker; deleting it later will not make it reappear on reload. Synthetic conversation data and the other five starter forms remain unchanged.
