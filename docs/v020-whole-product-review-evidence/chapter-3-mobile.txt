Broader coverage becomes attractive

Structured, batched questions and Jev’s very low model input cost make broader evaluation economically attractive.

In this example, a policy selects 24 of 48 eligible conversations (50%) from one customer-service queue. Each uses the same published form. Conditional questions need another request only when they apply.

The calculator on Welcome lets you change volume, selection and request assumptions. Model cost is one component of a pilot’s operating cost.
